Administrative Division

Matangai Payam

United under one banner, serving the people through four distinct directions.

North: Nyangkot
South: Cholochok
East: Matangai
West: Aber

Matangai Payam stands as the central pillar of the Nyang Community administration. It is comprised of four strategic Bomas positioned in the four cardinal directions, ensuring comprehensive governance and representation for every citizen.

West
01 / The Western Gate

Aber Boma

Located to the West

Situated to the West of Matangai Payam, Aber serves as a vital entry point and agricultural hub. The lands here are fertile, supporting the community's agrarian economy.

Under the guidance of its Executive Chief, Aber maintains peace and order, acting as a buffer and a trading post for the western regions.

North
02 / The Northern Reach

Nyangkot Boma

Located to the North

To the North lies Nyangkot, a region rich in cultural history and tradition. It connects Matangai Payam to the northern territories and is known for its vibrant community gatherings.

Nyangkot plays a critical role in preserving the cultural heritage of the Nyang people, ensuring that the customs of the north are passed down to future generations.

South
03 / The Southern Border

Cholochok Boma

Located to the South

Located to the South, Cholochok acts as the guardian of the Payam's southern borders. It is a region of resilience, where the community works together to secure the grazing lands and water resources.

The leadership in Cholochok is focused on conflict resolution and maintaining strong relationships with neighboring communities to the south.

East
04 / The Eastern Rise

Matangai Boma

Located to the East

To the East lies the namesake Boma, Matangai. As the central administrative heart of the Payam, it hosts the majority of the Payam's official offices and the Regional Courts.

Matangai Boma is the beacon of development, often spearheading new educational initiatives and infrastructure projects for the entire Payam.

Payam Administration

Matangai Payam is governed by a structured leadership system designed to serve justice and maintain order efficiently.

2 Regional Courts

Ensuring justice and legal compliance across the Payam.

4 Executive Chiefs

Leading the four Bomas: Aber, Nyangkot, Cholochok, and Matangai.

8 Sub-Chiefs

Assisting leadership at the village and clan levels.
