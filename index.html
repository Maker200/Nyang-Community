    Who We Are

    The Heart of Nyang

    The Nyang Community brings together the people of Nyang-Community to foster unity, preserve our cultural heritage, and drive sustainable development. Nyang Community, usually referred to as Nyang Machardit or Nyang Kuei, is one of the resilient communities that make up Rumbek Central County. It is comprised of three sections, namely Dor, Yom and Awan under Kuei Division of Agaan whose economic livelihoods depend on crops and animal production.

    The community has many schools, churches, and healthcare centers. Agriculture and cattle keeping are the main economic activities. Despite challenges such as flooding, poor road networks, and unemployment, the people remain united and committed to peace, development, and education.

    Geographical Location

    Nyang Community is located in Rumbek Central County in Lakes State, Republic of South Sudan. It lies within the greater Bhar el Ghazal region. The area is characterized by flat plains, seasonal rivers, and grasslands suitable for cattle grazing and farming.

    Cultural Practices

    The people of Nyang Community highly value their cultural identity. Traditional dances, songs, storytelling, wrestling, and initiation ceremonies are important parts of community life. Respect for elders and communal cooperation are strongly encouraged.

    NB. Nyang Community is rich in culture, unity, and resilience. Despite the many challenges facing the community, the people continue to work together toward peace, education, and development.
